## Description (≤4000 chars)

HazardNet gives Bangladesh residents fast, offline-first multi-hazard warnings across all 64 districts. Floods, cyclones, cold waves, heat waves, nor'westers, drought, and fire alerts appear on your lock screen and on a tappable division map.

- 64-district coverage with severity cards (Normal / Watch / Warning / Severe)
- Save home, work, and other places to get alerts specific to them
- Tap-to-call emergency numbers (999, Fire 16163, Flood 1090, Health 16263) even offline
- Works on airplane mode — alerts are cached on your device
- One-tap crowd-sourced reports (optional)
- In-app Bengali and English
- Large, high-contrast, 48dp buttons designed for thumb-zone use

**HazardNet is NOT an official warning service.** Please follow official bulletins from the Bangladesh Meteorological Department, the Flood Forecasting and Warning Centre, the Department of Agricultural Extension, and local authorities. During emergencies, call 999.
