# SKILL: WHO Post-Disaster Health Protocols
## OBJECTIVE
Trigger health advisories when HazardNet Severity > 0.65 (High Impact).

## FLOOD / CYCLONE HEALTH RISKS
1. **Waterborne Diseases (Cholera, Typhoid, Diarrhea):**
   - *Advisory:* "Boil all drinking water or use WHO-approved Aquatabs (1 tablet per 20L, wait 30 mins)."
   - *Action:* UNICEF/DPHE to deploy emergency tube-well testing for fecal contamination.
2. **Vector-Borne (Dengue, Malaria):**
   - *Advisory:* Stagnant water post-flood breeds Aedes mosquitoes. Use long-lasting insecticidal nets (LLINs) and clear standing puddles near homesteads.
3. **Skin & Eye Infections:**
   - *Advisory:* Avoid wading in stagnant floodwater without rubber boots. Wash with soap and clean tube-well water immediately if exposed.

## COLD WAVE HEALTH RISKS
- *Advisory:* Vulnerable populations (children <5, elderly) must stay indoors. Keep warm clothing dry. Monitor closely for acute respiratory infection (ARI) or pneumonia symptoms.
