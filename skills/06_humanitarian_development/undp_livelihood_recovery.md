# SKILL: UNDP Livelihood Recovery & Anticipatory Action
## OBJECTIVE
Implement Cash-for-Work, shock-responsive social protection, and early action intervention triggers.

## RECOVERY PROTOCOLS
- **Anticipatory Cash Transfers (BISP/Mobile Money):** Disburse mobile cash transfers 3 days prior to peak flood crest to allow vulnerable households to evacuate assets.
- **Cash-for-Work (CfW):** Mobilize local labor for post-disaster embankment repairs, canal clearing, and village road reconstruction.
- **Agricultural Asset Replacement:** Provide smallholder farmers with ducklings, goats, vegetable seed packs, and micro-tillage equipment.
