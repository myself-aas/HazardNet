# SKILL: World Bank Catastrophe Risk & Parametric Insurance
## OBJECTIVE
Align hazard severity triggers with parametric disaster financing mechanisms (e.g., Bangladesh Climate Change Resilience Fund, World Bank CAT-DDO).

## TRIGGER CONDITIONS
- **Parametric Flood Index:** Inundation > 25% of district cultivated area for > 7 consecutive days automatically activates parametric payout mechanisms to regional microfinance institutions.
- **Crop Loss Ratio:** Modeled yield reduction > 40% triggers government agricultural loan waivers and subsidized interest rate refinancing.
