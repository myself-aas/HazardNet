# SKILL: UNICEF Child WASH & Protection Protocols
## OBJECTIVE
Safeguard children, pregnant women, and lactating mothers during extreme flood/cyclone events.

## ACTIONABLE PROTOCOLS
- **WASH Kits:** Distribute emergency WASH kits containing water purification tablets, soap, collapsible jerry cans, and dignity items.
- **Child-Friendly Spaces (CFS):** Establish safe, elevated spaces in flood shelters to prevent child drowning and operationalize psychosocial support.
- **Micro-Nutrient Supplementation:** Provide Ready-to-Use Therapeutic Food (RUTF) to prevent acute malnutrition during prolonged crop destruction.
