# SKILL: ERA5 Reanalysis Climate Drivers
## OBJECTIVE
Interpret atmospheric, surface temperature, precipitation, and soil layer ERA5 variables driving agricultural hazards in Bangladesh.

## DRIVER THRESHOLDS
- **Precipitation Anomaly:** Daily rainfall > 100mm/day in Meghalaya/Assam catchments triggers transboundary flash floods in Sylhet/Sunamganj within 24–48 hours.
- **Root Zone Soil Moisture (Soil_W3):** Volumetric water content < 0.15 m³/m³ in Barind Tract (Rajshahi, Dinajpur) signals deep agricultural drought.
- **Diurnal Thermal Extremes:** Max_Temp > 38°C (311.15K) accompanied by Solar_Rad > 22 MJ/m²/day causes heat blast / spikelet sterility in Boro rice at flowering stage.
