# SKILL: DAE Extension Standard Operating Procedure (Department of Agricultural Extension)
## OBJECTIVE
Guide Sub-Assistant Agriculture Officers (SAAO) and Upazila Agriculture Officers (UAO) in field triage, damage reporting, and emergency input distribution.

## SAAO FIELD ACTION STEPS
1. **Block Triage:** Conduct physical inspection within 24 hours of hazard occurrence across all designated agricultural blocks.
2. **Loss Assessment:** Report damaged area (hectares), affected farmers, and crop stage via DAE Krishok App / Form-A to UAO.
3. **Input Distribution:** Coordinate emergency distribution of BRRI/BARI seeds, fertilizer kits, and mechanical harvesters (Combine Harvesters).
