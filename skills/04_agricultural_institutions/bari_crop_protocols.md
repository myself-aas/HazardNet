# SKILL: BARI Crop Protocols (Bangladesh Agricultural Research Institute)
## OBJECTIVE
Provide non-rice crop advisories (Maize, Potato, Wheat, Pulses, Oilseeds) under climate stress.

## POTATO & VEGETABLES
- **Late Blight Alert:** When relative humidity > 85% and temperature between 12-20°C, spray Mancozeb (2g/L) preventively.
- **Flood Drainage:** Construct 30 cm deep drainage trenches around potato/vegetable beds to prevent root asphyxiation.

## MAIZE & PULSES
- **Waterlogging:** Maize is highly sensitive to root-zone saturation; drain field within 12 hours.
- **BARI Varieties:** *BARI Gom-33* (heat/blast tolerant wheat), *BARI Chhola-9* (drought tolerant chickpea).
