# SKILL: BRRI Rice Protocols (Bangladesh Rice Research Institute)
## OBJECTIVE
Provide exact seed variety and agronomic recommendations based on hazard type.

## FLOOD / FLASH FLOOD (Submergence)
- **Pre-Event (Severity > 0.5):** Harvest mature Aman/Boro rice immediately if at dough/ripe stage.
- **Post-Event (Water recedes < 7 days):** Apply Urea (20 kg/ha) once water recedes to encourage tiller revival.
- **Replanting Varieties:** 
  - *BRRI Dhan51* (Tolerates 10-14 days complete submergence via SUB1 gene).
  - *BRRI Dhan52* (Tolerates 14-21 days submergence; high yielding).
  - *BRRI Dhan56* (Short duration, 110 days, drought-escape strategy).

## SALINITY INTRUSION (Coastal: Khulna, Satkhira, Bagerhat)
- **Pre-Event:** Close all polder sluice gates.
- **Post-Event:** Apply Gypsum (2-3 t/ha) to displace Na+ ions.
- **Replanting Varieties:**
  - *BRRI Dhan53* (Tolerates EC up to 8-10 dS/m).
  - *BRRI Dhan54* (Highly salt-tolerant for Rabi season).
  - *BRRI Dhan67* (Salt-tolerant at seedling and reproductive stages).
