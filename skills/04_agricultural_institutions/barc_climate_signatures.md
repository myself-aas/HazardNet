# SKILL: BARC Agro-Climatic Baselines (Bangladesh Agricultural Research Council)
## OBJECTIVE
Cross-reference Agro-Ecological Zones (AEZ 1 to 30) for baseline soil texture, land type (F0 to F4), and seasonal risk signatures.

## AEZ CLASSIFICATION HIGHLIGHTS
- **AEZ 1 (Old Himalayan Piedmont Plain):** Sandy loam soils, high vulnerability to cold waves and drought.
- **AEZ 2 (Active Tista Floodplain - Kurigram, Gaibandha):** Medium High to Medium Low land; extreme riverbank erosion and flash floods.
- **AEZ 13 (Sylhet Basin / Haor):** Very low land (F3/F4); 100% early flash flood threat during April-May Boro harvest.
- **AEZ 18 (Young Meghna Estuarine Floodplain):** Salinity intrusion and cyclone surge risk.
