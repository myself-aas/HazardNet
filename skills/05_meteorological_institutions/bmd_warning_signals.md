# SKILL: BMD Warning Signal Mapping (Bangladesh Meteorological Department)
## OBJECTIVE
Align HazardNet predictions with official Bangladesh Meteorological Department (BMD) signals for public trust and institutional compliance.

## CYCLONE WARNING SIGNALS
- **Distant Cautionary Signal 1:** HazardNet Severity 0.25 - 0.40. *Action:* Keep tracking storm track in Bay of Bengal.
- **Local Warning Signal 3:** HazardNet Severity 0.41 - 0.65. *Action:* Ports warned, fishing boats return to shore, farmers prepare for wind gusts.
- **Danger Signal 5 - 7:** HazardNet Severity 0.66 - 0.80. *Action:* Evacuate coastal lowlands to cyclone shelters; secure livestock.
- **Great Danger Signal 8 - 10:** HazardNet Severity > 0.80. *Action:* Extreme crisis; total lockdown; full disaster response.

## HEAVY RAINFALL / FLASH FLOOD ALERTS
- **Yellow Alert:** Rainfall 44 - 88 mm/24hr. Clearing drainage channels.
- **Red Alert:** Rainfall > 88 mm/24hr. Immediate harvest and highland relocation.
