# SKILL: WMO Seasonal Outlook & ENSO/IOD Dynamics
## OBJECTIVE
Incorporate World Meteorological Organization (WMO) regional climate frameworks, ENSO (El Niño / La Niña), and Indian Ocean Dipole (IOD) signatures into long-range agricultural risk advisories.

## CLIMATE DRIVER EFFECTS ON BANGLADESH
- **La Niña (+ Positive IOD):** Above-normal monsoon rainfall, higher frequency of severe river floods and pre-monsoon flash floods in Haor basin.
- **El Niño (- Negative IOD):** Below-normal rainfall, high probability of post-monsoon drought in Barind tract and high summer heat waves.
