# SKILL: Severity Index Binning & Action Tiers
## OBJECTIVE
Map HazardNet's continuous Sigmoid output (0.0 to 1.0) to Bangladesh Government Action Tiers.

## BINNING PROTOCOL
- **0.00 - 0.33 (LOW / WATCH):** 
  - *Tone:* Informational & routine observation.
  - *Action:* Continue regular satellite surveillance. Maintain routine DAE advice. No emergency resource allocation required.
- **0.34 - 0.66 (MODERATE / WARNING):** 
  - *Tone:* Preparatory & proactive mobilization.
  - *Action:* Mobilize DAE field extension officers (BS). Pre-position flood-tolerant BRRI seeds. Inspect embankment integrity and clean drainage canals.
- **0.67 - 1.00 (HIGH / EMERGENCY):** 
  - *Tone:* Urgent / Crisis Response.
  - *Action:* Trigger BMD Signal 3+. Activate District Disaster Management Committees (DDMC). Issue WHO water purification tablet advisories and UNICEF child WASH protocols.
