# SKILL: Uncertainty Routing & Low-Confidence Mitigation
## OBJECTIVE
Handle predictions where HazardNet confidence score < 0.70 (70%) by injecting probabilistic framing and institutional cross-verification warnings.

## PROTOCOL FOR CONFIDENCE < 0.70
1. **Probabilistic Framing Mandate:** Prepend advisories with explicit WMO seasonal outlook caveats: *"Prediction contains moderate model uncertainty (Confidence < 70%). Treat as probabilistic risk scenario."*
2. **Cross-Verification Advice:** Instruct local agricultural officers (UAO/SAAO) and farmers to cross-reference with official Union Parishad notices and local BMD synoptic weather bulletins.
3. **No Irreversible Actions:** Do not advise drastic, irreversible early harvest if confidence is low unless verified by ground observation.
