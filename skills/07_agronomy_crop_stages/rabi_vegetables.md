# SKILL: Rabi Crops & Winter Vegetables Phenology
## OBJECTIVE
Map winter non-rice crops (Potato, Mustard, Wheat, Tomato, Pulses) to cold wave, late blight, and unseasonal rainfall hazards.

## AGRONOMIC GUIDELINES
- **Potato (Nov - Feb):** Late Blight fungal attack during fog/cold wave. Spray preventive copper oxychloride or Mancozeb.
- **Mustard (Oct - Jan):** Aphid infestation during warm winter snaps. Apply bio-pesticides or neem oil.
- **Wheat (Nov - Mar):** Wheat Blast disease during humid warm spell. Use *BARI Gom-33*.
