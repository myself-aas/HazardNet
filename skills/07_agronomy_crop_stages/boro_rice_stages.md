# SKILL: Boro Rice Agronomic Phenology & Hazard Vulnerability
## OBJECTIVE
Map winter/spring irrigated Boro rice phenological stages to cold waves, pre-monsoon flash floods, and heat waves.

## PHENOLOGICAL STAGES & THREATS
- **Seedbed (November - December):** Cold wave injury (< 10°C). *Mitigation:* Water inundation at night, polythene covering.
- **Transplanting (December - January):** Cold shock. *Mitigation:* Avoid transplanting during peak cold wave.
- **Vegetative / Booting (February - March):** Water stress. *Mitigation:* Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD).
- **Flowering / Panicle (March - April):** Heat wave (>35°C) & Kalbaishakhi hail storms. *Mitigation:* Maintain standing water layer.
- **Harvest (April - May):** Haor flash floods in Sylhet/Sunamganj/Kurigram. *Mitigation:* Harvest at 80% ripeness using combine harvesters.
