# SKILL: Aman Rice Agronomic Phenology & Hazard Vulnerability
## OBJECTIVE
Map monsoon-season Aman rice phenological stages to seasonal climate threats (Monsoon floods, late-season drought, coastal salinity).

## PHENOLOGICAL STAGES & THREATS
- **Seedbed (June - July):** Submergence risk from early monsoon rain. *Mitigation:* Floating seedbeds (*Baira*).
- **Transplanting (July - August):** High flood risk. *Mitigation:* Use 30-35 day old seedlings of *BRRI Dhan51/52*.
- **Vegetative / Tillering (August - September):** Submergence & stem borer attack. *Mitigation:* Apply balanced N-P-K fertilizer.
- **Flowering / Panicle Initiation (September - October):** Moisture stress / early drought (*Kharif-II drought*). *Mitigation:* Supplementary irrigation from local water bodies.
- **Harvest (November - December):** Cyclonic rain / lodging. *Mitigation:* Harvest at 80% grain yellowing.
