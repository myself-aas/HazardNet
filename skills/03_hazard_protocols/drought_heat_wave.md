# SKILL: Agricultural Drought & Heat Wave Protocol
## OBJECTIVE
Combat root-zone water deficits and heat-induced spikelet sterility during severe dry spells in Barind and high-land zones.

## AGRONOMIC ADVISORIES
- **Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD):** Implement AWD irrigation technique in Boro paddy fields to reduce water consumption by 30% without yield penalty.
- **Heat Blast Suppression:** Maintain 2-3 cm standing water in paddy fields during flowering/panicle initiation stage to buffer canopy temperatures against extreme heat (>36°C).
- **Mulching:** Apply straw or plastic mulching in maize, potato, and vegetable plots to conserve soil moisture (Soil_W3).
- **Drought-Tolerant Varieties:** Recommend *BRRI Dhan56*, *BRRI Dhan66*, and BARI Drought-Tolerant Wheat/Maize varieties.
