# SKILL: Flood & Flash Flood Response Protocol
## OBJECTIVE
Deliver actionable crop protection, embankment monitoring, and post-inundation recovery advisories for flood and flash flood events in Bangladesh.

## PRE-EVENT ACTIONS (Severity > 0.50)
- **Harvest Priority:** Harvest mature Boro/Aman paddy immediately if crops are at 80%+ maturity (dough to ripe stage) to avoid total inundation loss.
- **Seedbed Protection:** Raise seedbed perimeters with bamboo fencing and polythene covers; prepare floating seedbeds (*Baira*) in low-lying Haor basins.
- **Livestock Safety:** Move cattle and poultry to elevated embankments (*Killa*) or highland shelters.

## POST-FLOOD RECOVERY (Water Recession)
- **Soil Fertility:** Apply 20 kg/ha Urea and 10 kg/ha MOP once floodwaters recede to restore submerged root vitality.
- **Replanting / Floating Seedbed Varieties:**
  - *BRRI Dhan51 / BRRI Dhan52:* Submergence-tolerant rice varieties for up to 14–21 days of total inundation.
  - *BRRI Dhan56 / BRRI Dhan57:* Short-duration varieties for late-season escape cropping.
