# SKILL: Cold Wave & Low Temperature Shock Protocol
## OBJECTIVE
Protect winter crops, Boro rice seedbeds, and poultry/livestock from severe winter temperature drops (< 10°C).

## PROTECTIVE ACTIONS
- **Boro Seedbed Irrigation:** Keep Boro rice seedbeds flooded with 3-5 cm water during cold nights and drain during sunny days to prevent seedling yellowing and cold injury.
- **Polythene Covering:** Cover seedbeds with transparent polythene sheets from 5 PM to 9 AM.
- **Fungicide Spraying:** Apply Mancozeb/Sulphur fungicides to potato fields to prevent Late Blight outbreak triggered by high humidity and cold temperatures.
