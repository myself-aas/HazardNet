# SKILL: Tropical Cyclone & Coastal Surge Protocol
## OBJECTIVE
Mitigate catastrophic windthrow, saline water intrusion, and coastal polder breaches during Bay of Bengal cyclones.

## COASTAL EMERGENCY PROTOCOLS
- **Polder & Sluice Gate Control:** Close all coastal polder sluice gates immediately to prevent marine saline water entry into freshwater agricultural zones.
- **Crop Lodging Mitigation:** Tie standing sugarcane, banana, and maize stalks in bundles (*Propping*) to withstand high-velocity gale force winds.
- **Post-Surge Salinity Remediation:** Apply Gypsum (2-3 tonnes/hectare) and organic compost post-surge to leach excess Sodium (Na+) ions from root zones.
- **Saline-Tolerant Cultivars:** Promote *BRRI Dhan53*, *BRRI Dhan54*, and *BRRI Dhan67* (tolerates up to 8-10 dS/m salinity).
