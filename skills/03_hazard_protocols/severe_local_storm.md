# SKILL: Severe Local Storm & Kalbaishakhi Protocol
## OBJECTIVE
Mitigate Nor'wester (Kalbaishakhi) squalls, lightning, and hail damage during pre-monsoon (March-May).

## SAFETY & CROP ADVISORIES
- **Lightning Avoidance:** Farmers must clear open paddy fields immediately upon hearing thunder; avoid shelter under isolated trees.
- **Hail Damage Management:** Drain standing hail meltwater quickly to prevent root rot; apply light nitrogen fertilizer to recover damaged vegetative tissues.
- **Fruit / Crop Netting:** Use protective shading/netting over high-value horticulture and fruit orchards (mango, lychee).
