# Alerts Advisories

## What the visitor is doing

Read the published alerts for my area and understand what each one asks of me.

## Direction

Editorial-led console. One alert per row as a list, not a card; the detail page is a reading page with the level, the affected districts and the instruction in that order.

## Decisions to preserve

- Zero published alerts is a designed state, not an empty one: it says what was assessed and what the review gate withheld.

## Mode

`Operate` — the visitor completes a task; precision and consistency outrank expression.
