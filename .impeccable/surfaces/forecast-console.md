# Forecast Console

## What the visitor is doing

Compare districts and horizons, and adjust what the console shows.

## Direction

Console track. Dense tables and charts on the ground; a chart is never a card with a shadow. Axes, ticks and reference lines come from the neutral ramp and the severity scale.

## Decisions to preserve

- Tabular numerals in every numeric column.
- Charts read in both grounds; the severity thresholds are labelled, not implied.

## Mode

`Operate` — the visitor completes a task; precision and consistency outrank expression.
