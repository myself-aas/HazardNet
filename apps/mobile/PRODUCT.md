# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

This file overrides the repo-root `PRODUCT.md` for `apps/mobile` only. It exists because one
inherited record cannot describe two platforms: the root record declares `web` (the shipped
site), while this workspace carries the Expo/React Native client, and inheriting `web` meant
the web guidance was applied to native code and the iOS/Android references never loaded.
`DESIGN.md` still inherits from the root — the visual system is shared.

## Platform

adaptive

## Stack

Expo SDK 51 on React Native 0.74, TypeScript, React Navigation (native stack + bottom tabs),
TanStack Query with an AsyncStorage persister, `@react-native-async-storage`, NetInfo,
`@shopify/flash-list`, Reanimated 3, expo-notifications/location/haptics/file-system.
Reads `@hazardnet/core` and `@hazardnet/design-system` from the workspace. Source only:
`android/` and `ios/` are generated by `expo prebuild` and are git-ignored.

## Users

The two field audiences from the root record, on the devices they actually carry:

1. **Farmers and rural residents in Bangladesh** — low-end Android handsets, 2G/3G, metered
   data, direct sunlight, Bangla.
2. **Agricultural extension workers and union parishad officials** — older or shared
   devices, intermittent connectivity, needing something they can act on today.

The desktop, research and reviewer audiences of the root record are not this client's
audience; they read the site.

## Product Purpose

The same published outlook as the site, sized for a phone in the field: today's alerts and
advisories, a map, saved districts, and one honest status line. Nothing here computes a
forecast of its own — it reads the artifacts the pipeline published.

## Positioning

A companion to the site, not a second product. It exists because the visitor's device and
network, not their interest, rule the site out. It is not a lighter site and not an
independent app with its own truth: when the two disagree, the artifacts are wrong for both.

## Operating Context

Offline-first by necessity: the query cache persists to AsyncStorage, NetInfo drives a
foreground banner when connectivity drops, and notifications carry an alert to a phone that
may be asleep. Bangla is a first-class reading language, and a session may last seconds
before the data bundle is switched off.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Hard constraint:** there are no Android signing secrets in this repository, so the
  release pipeline is parked at
  `.github/workflow-templates/hazardnet-mobile-android-release.yml`. Nothing publishes an
  APK or AAB on push. Do not add a live workflow back without the keystore.
- No compiled binaries are committed; executables came from CI and live on GitHub Releases.
- The app may not claim a warning the pipeline did not publish. Official warnings come from
  BMD and FFWC; emergencies go to 999.

## Brand Commitments

Inherits `DESIGN.md` (Cupertino Precision) — Apple's type ramp, the HDS palette, one action
colour, depth as a hairline. On a native client the platform's own conventions win where
they conflict with the web expression: navigation, back gesture, sheets, haptics, and the
system's safe areas are not restyled into web patterns.

## Evidence on Hand

- `docs/APP_RELEASES.md` — what ships, how it was built, and the restore checklist for both
  parked templates.
- `apps/mobile/src/screens/{today,alerts,advisories,map,saved,report,articles,more,
  onboarding,settings}` and `src/navigation/RootNavigator.tsx` — the surface as built.
- `.maestro/today-alerts-flow.yaml` — the one scripted end-to-end flow.
- The artifact contracts in `/data` (`alerts-latest.json`, `freshness.json`), shared with
  the site.

## Product Principles

1. The artifact is the truth; the client renders it and says when it cannot.
2. Offline is a state, not an error: say what was cached, dated when.
3. One decision per screen. A farmer reading in sunlight is not browsing.
4. Bangla and English are the same product, not a translation layer.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

44×44 minimum targets, platform accessibility labels on every control, Dynamic Type /
font-scale support without clipping, reduced-motion honoured for the Reanimated transitions,
and contrast measured against the real surfaces rather than the design tokens alone.
