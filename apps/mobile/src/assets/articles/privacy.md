# Privacy

HazardNet is designed to keep your data on your device.

## What stays on your phone
- Saved places (Home, Work, family villages).
- Current-location fixes (used only for on-device matching unless you opt in to sync).
- Field reports and photos (stay queued locally until they upload).
- Notification preferences and quiet hours.

## What leaves your phone (only if you opt in)
- Anonymous error reports (no PII).
- If you create an account in a future release: encrypted, end-to-end backed-up saved places.
- Field reports you explicitly submit, including any photo you attach and (if enabled) the location tag.

## What we never collect
- Precise location in analytics.
- Contact lists, messages, camera roll metadata beyond the photo you attach.
- Advertising identifiers.
