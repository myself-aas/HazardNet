# BRRI Stress-Tolerant Rice Varieties Database
## Used for Post-Hazard Replanting Recommendations

### Submergence Tolerant (Flood)
- **BRRI Dhan51**: Tolerates submergence up to 10-12 days. (Kharif-II)
- **BRRI Dhan52**: Tolerates submergence up to 2 weeks. (Kharif-II)
- **BRRI Dhan56**: Short duration (110 days), good for flash flood recovery.

### Drought Tolerant (Rainfed Aus/Upland)
- **BRRI Dhan57**: Deep rooting, drought-tolerant. (Aus)
- **BRRI Dhan61**: Tolerates moisture stress at reproductive stage.
- **BRRI Dhan71**: Upland rice, suitable for Barind tract.

### Salinity Tolerant (Coastal)
- **BRRI Dhan53**: Tolerates EC up to 8-10 dS/m. (Kharif-II)
- **BRRI Dhan54**: Highly salt-tolerant, suitable for Rabi season in coastal belts.
- **BRRI Dhan55**: Tolerant to tidal surge salinity.

### Cold Tolerant (Boro Seedling Stage)
- **BRRI Dhan36**: Tolerates low temperature at seedling stage.
- **BRRI Dhan62**: Cold tolerant for late Rabi planting.
