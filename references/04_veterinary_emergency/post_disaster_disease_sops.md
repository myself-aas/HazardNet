# VETERINARY EMERGENCY PROTOCOLS (DLS)
## Triggered ONLY when HazardNet Severity >= 0.65 (High/Severe)

### 1. Anthrax & Black Quarter (Post-Flood)
- **Trigger:** Flooding followed by sudden cattle deaths with dark blood from natural orifices.
- **SOP:** DO NOT open carcasses (prevents spore formation). Bury deep (6 ft) with quicklime. Ring-vaccinate all healthy cattle within a 10km radius with Anthrax Spore Vaccine (Department of Livestock Services).

### 2. Foot and Mouth Disease (FMD) & Hemorrhagic Septicemia (Post-Cold Wave/Transition)
- **Trigger:** Cold Wave or sudden temperature drops affecting immunocompromised livestock.
- **SOP:** Isolate infected animals immediately. Provide soft, easily digestible fodder and potassium permanganate mouth washes. Vaccinate with Polyvalent FMD vaccine.

### 3. Avian Influenza & Newcastle Disease (Post-Cyclone/Flood)
- **Trigger:** Poultry sheds damaged, wild migratory birds mixing with domestic flocks due to habitat loss.
- **SOP:** Cull infected flocks safely under DLS supervision (PPE required). Disinfect sheds with 2% sodium hypochlorite. Ban movement of live poultry from the 3km containment zone.
