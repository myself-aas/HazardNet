# SECTOR PROFILE: LIVESTOCK & POULTRY (DLS & BLRI Protocols)

### 1. Cattle & Goat (e.g., Sirajganj, Kurigram, Jamalpur, Pabna)
- **Primary Hazard:** Riverine Flood, Flash Flood.
- **Vulnerability:** Drowning, loss of shelter, severe post-flood fodder crisis, waterborne diseases (bloat, foot rot).
- **Pre-Event Action (48h):** Evacuate cattle to elevated cyclone/flood shelters (Machrang / Killa) or high-ground community pastures. Stockpile dry fodder (hay, straw, urea-molasses treated straw) for 15 days.
- **Post-Event Recovery:** Provide emergency fodder blocks and mineral licks. Treat for foot rot with 5% copper sulphate foot baths. Ensure access to clean, arsenic/contamination-free tube-well water.

### 2. Poultry (Broiler & Layer - Nationwide, highly sensitive to Heatwaves & Floods)
- **Primary Hazard:** Heat Wave, Cold Wave, Flood (shed destruction).
- **Vulnerability:** Broilers suffer massive mortality at >35°C. Floods destroy low-lying sheds and contaminate feed with mycotoxins.
- **Pre-Event Action (Heatwave):** Provide electrolytes (Vitamin C, Potassium Chloride) in drinking water. Install foggers/sprinklers on shed roofs. Reduce stocking density by 20%.
- **Pre-Event Action (Flood):** Relocate birds to elevated shelters. Store feed on plastic pallets above flood level in airtight drums.
