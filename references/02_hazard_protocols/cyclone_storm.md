# Hazard Protocol: Tropical Cyclone & Severe Local Storm
## Context: Coastal & Haor Regions (Satkhira, Khulna, Barisal, Sylhet)
### Immediate Actions (0-48 Hours)
- **Embankments**: Sandbag vulnerable points on polders. Close all sluice gates (Sluice Board protocols) to prevent tidal surge intrusion.
- **Propping**: Bamboo propping for tall crops (Banana, Sugarcane, late Aman rice).
- **Harvest**: Harvest mature crops (Potato, Vegetables, mature Aman) immediately.
- **Shelters**: Evacuate livestock and humans to designated Cyclone Shelters (CCB).

### Protective Measures (1-2 Weeks)
- **Saltwater Flushing**: If tidal surge breaches embankments, immediately pump or channel freshwater to flush salt from soil profiles before it penetrates deeper.
- **Debris Clearing**: Remove salt-contaminated debris from agricultural lands to prevent secondary salinization.

### Contingency & Recovery
- **Salinity Spike**: Apply agricultural gypsum (2-3 tons/ha) to displace sodium ions if soil EC > 4 dS/m.
- **Replanting**: Use salt-tolerant varieties (BR-11, BRRI Dhan53, BRRI Dhan54) or switch to sunflower/maize in severely affected coastal belts.
