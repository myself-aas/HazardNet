# Hazard Protocol: Flood & Flash Flood
## Context: Bangladesh Deltaic Agriculture
### Immediate Actions (0-48 Hours)
- **Drainage**: Clear blocked drainage channels and culverts using community labor (Kazal/Shramik).
- **Harvest**: If Aman rice is at dough/mature stage and flash flood is imminent, harvest immediately even if moisture content is high (use tarpaulins for drying).
- **Livestock**: Move cattle and poultry to elevated cyclone shelters or raised platforms (Machrang).
- **Seed Storage**: Move stored seeds (BRRI varieties) and fertilizers to waterproof, elevated rooms.

### Protective Measures (1-2 Weeks)
- **Post-Flood Drainage**: Dig peripheral trenches (1.5 ft deep) around fields to drain stagnant water within 3-5 days to prevent root rot.
- **Disease Management**: Spray fungicides (e.g., Tilt 250 EC @ 1ml/L) if bacterial leaf blight or sheath blight symptoms appear post-recession.
- **Nutrient Recovery**: Apply urea top-dressing (20-30 kg/ha) once water recedes to stimulate tillering recovery.

### Contingency & Recovery
- **Crop Loss >70%**: Transition to short-duration crops (e.g., BRRI Dhan56, Moong bean, or quick-growing vegetables like Spinach/Lalshak) using residual soil moisture.
- **Fisheries**: Restock flooded beels/haors with carp fingerlings once water quality stabilizes.
