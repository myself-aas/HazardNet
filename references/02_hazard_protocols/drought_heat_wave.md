# Hazard Protocol: Drought & Heat Wave
## Context: Barind Tract (Rajshahi, Rangpur) & Pre-Monsoon (Aus Season)
### Immediate Actions (0-48 Hours)
- **AWD Technique**: Implement Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) in Boro rice. Use a simple PVC pipe (Tensiometer) to irrigate only when water level drops 15cm below soil surface. Saves 30% water.
- **Mulching**: Apply straw mulch (5-7 cm) around potato and vegetable bases to reduce soil evaporation.
- **Water Scheduling**: Irrigate during early morning (6 AM) or late evening (5 PM) to minimize evaporative loss.

### Protective Measures (1-2 Weeks)
- **Heat Stress Mitigation**: Spray water on vegetable canopies (cucurbitaceae, solanaceae) during peak heat (12 PM - 3 PM).
- **Nutrient Adjustment**: Reduce nitrogenous fertilizers (which promote lush, water-demanding growth); increase Potash (MOP) to improve plant cell turgor and drought resistance.

### Contingency & Recovery
- **Crop Failure**: If Boro rice fails at panicle initiation, plow and plant short-duration pulses (Masoor, Chickpea) or Maize if residual moisture permits.
