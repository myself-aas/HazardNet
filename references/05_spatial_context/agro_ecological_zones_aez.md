# Agro-Ecological Zones (AEZ) Reference
## Based on FAO & BARC (Bangladesh Agricultural Research Council)

| AEZ Name | Dominant Districts | Soil Type | Primary Crops | Hazard Susceptibility |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tidal Floodplain** | Khulna, Satkhira, Patuakhali | Saline, Alluvial | Aman Rice, Sunflower, Pulses | Cyclone, Salinity, Surge |
| **Ganges River Floodplain** | Rajshahi, Pabna, Kushtia | Loam, Silty Loam | Jute, Rice, Wheat, Sugarcane | River Flood, Drought |
| **Barind Tract** | Rajshahi, Rangpur, Dinajpur | Red Clay, Hardpan | Boro Rice, Potato, Maize, Mango | Severe Drought, Heat Wave |
| **Haor Basin** | Sunamganj, Sylhet, Netrokona | Hydromorphic, Clay | Boro Rice, Aus Rice, Fish | Flash Flood (Pre-monsoon) |
| **Active Meghna Floodplain** | Comilla, Noakhali, Bhola | Sandy, Loamy | Aman Rice, Jute, Pulses | River Erosion, Monsoon Flood |
| **Chittagong Hill Tracts** | Rangamati, Bandarban, Khagrachari | Red Loam, Hilly | Jhum crops, Banana, Vegetables | Landslide, Flash Flood, Storm |
| **Karatoa Atrai Floodplain** | Bogra, Joypurhat, Dinajpur | Loam, Sandy Loam | Potato, Wheat, Rice, Tobacco | Winter Cold Wave, Drought |
