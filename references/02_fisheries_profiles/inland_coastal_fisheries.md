# SECTOR PROFILE: FISHERIES (DoF & BFRI Protocols)
## Context: Bangladesh is the 3rd largest inland fish producer globally.

### 1. Inland Open Water (Haors & Beels - e.g., Sunamganj, Mymensingh, Netrokona, Natore)
- **Primary Hazard:** Flash Floods (Pre-monsoon), River Erosion.
- **Vulnerability:** Sudden water level rises wash away fish from open waters; destroys cage culture infrastructure.
- **Pre-Event Action (48h):** Harvest mature fish (Rui, Katla, Ilish, Pangasius) immediately. Secure floating cage nets with heavy anchors.
- **Post-Event Recovery:** Restock with fast-growing fingerlings (Tilapia, Silver Carp) provided by Department of Fisheries (DoF). Apply lime (CaCO3 at 250 kg/ha) to stabilize water pH and clear turbidity after floodwater recedes.

### 2. Coastal Aquaculture (Shrimp Gher/Mouth - e.g., Satkhira, Khulna, Bagerhat, Cox's Bazar)
- **Primary Hazard:** Tropical Cyclone, Tidal Surge, Salinity Intrusion.
- **Vulnerability:** Embankment breaches flush out shrimp/fish; extreme salinity spikes cause mass mortality in freshwater prawns (Golda).
- **Pre-Event Action (48h):** Lower pond water levels by 20% to prevent wave overtopping. Reinforce earthen embankments with bamboo nets and sandbags. Close polder sluice gates.
- **Post-Event Recovery:** Repair breached sluice gates immediately. Flush ponds with freshwater if available. Apply agricultural gypsum (2 t/ha) to pond soils to bind excess sodium (Na+).
