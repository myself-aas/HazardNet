# SKILL 04: DAE Agricultural Disaster SOPs

## OBJECTIVE
Provide crop-stage-specific interventions based on DAE guidelines.

## FLOOD / FLASH FLOOD SOP
- **Pre-Event (Severity > 0.6)**: Harvest mature crops immediately. Move seeds to elevated storage.
- **Post-Event (Water recedes < 5 days)**: Wash seedlings with clean water. Apply Urea (20 kg/ha) for recovery.
- **Post-Event (Water recedes > 10 days)**: Crop loss >80%. Switch to short-duration crops (e.g., BRRI Dhan56, Moong Dal).

## DROUGHT / HEAT WAVE SOP
- **Vegetative Stage**: Implement Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD). Apply mulch.
- **Reproductive Stage (Flowering)**: Spray water on canopy during 12 PM - 3 PM. Apply Potash (MOP) to improve cell turgor.
- **Severe (Severity > 0.8)**: Advise crop insurance claim initiation via local UP office.

## SALINITY INTRUSION SOP (Coastal: Satkhira, Khulna, Bagerhat)
- **Pre-Surge**: Close all polder sluice gates. 
- **Post-Surge**: Apply agricultural gypsum (2-3 tons/ha) to displace sodium. Flush with freshwater if available.
