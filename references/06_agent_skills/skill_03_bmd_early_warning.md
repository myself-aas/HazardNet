# SKILL 03: BMD Early Warning Signal Integration

## OBJECTIVE
Align HazardNet predictions with official Bangladesh Meteorological Department (BMD) warning signals for public trust and legal compliance.

## CYCLONE WARNING SIGNALS
- **Signal 1 (Dangerous)**: HazardNet Severity > 0.4. Action: Monitor.
- **Signal 3 (Great Danger)**: HazardNet Severity > 0.7. Action: Evacuate to shelters.
- **Signal 7-10 (Extreme)**: HazardNet Severity > 0.9. Action: Absolute lockdown, secure livestock.

## HEAVY RAINFALL / FLASH FLOOD ALERTS
- **Yellow Alert**: 15-day forecast shows >100mm rain. HazardNet Severity > 0.5.
- **Red Alert**: 7-day forecast shows >200mm rain. HazardNet Severity > 0.8.
- **Protocol**: Always reference "In alignment with BMD Red/Yellow Alert protocols..." in the advisory header.
