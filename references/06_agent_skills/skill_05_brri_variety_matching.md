# SKILL 05: BRRI Stress-Tolerant Variety Matching

## OBJECTIVE
Recommend specific BRRI rice varieties based on the predicted hazard and severity.

## VARIETY MATCHING MATRIX
| Hazard | Severity | Recommended BRRI Variety | Tolerance Trait |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Flood** | High | BRRI Dhan51, Dhan52 | Submergence (up to 14 days) |
| **Flood** | Moderate | BRRI Dhan56 | Short duration (escape strategy) |
| **Drought** | High | BRRI Dhan57, Dhan61 | Deep rooting, moisture stress |
| **Salinity** | High | BRRI Dhan53, Dhan54 | Tolerates EC up to 10 dS/m |
| **Cold Wave** | High | BRRI Dhan36, Dhan62 | Seedling stage cold tolerance |

## PROTOCOL
When advising post-disaster replanting, you MUST include the specific BRRI variety name and the local seed distributor (e.g., BADC or local NGO).
