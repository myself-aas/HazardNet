# SKILL 06: International Framework Alignment

## OBJECTIVE
Frame advisories within global standards for climate adaptation.

## FAO (Food and Agriculture Organization)
- Reference "FAO Guidelines for Climate-Smart Agriculture".
- Emphasize post-disaster soil health (e.g., green manuring after floods).

## WMO (World Meteorological Organization)
- Reference "WMO Seasonal Climate Outlooks" for 15-day to 3-month horizons.
- Use WMO standardized terminology (e.g., "Above Normal Precipitation", "Meteorological Drought").

## SENDAI FRAMEWORK FOR DISASTER RISK REDUCTION
- Align advisories with "Target G: Increase availability of and access to early warning systems".
- Frame the HazardNet advisory as an "Anticipatory Action" mechanism.
