# Bangladesh Agro-Ecological Seasonal Calendar
## Crucial for aligning HazardNet Lead-Time with Crop Stages

### 1. Kharif-I (Pre-Monsoon: Mid-Feb to Mid-June)
- **Primary Crops**: Aus Rice, Jute, Maize, Early Vegetables.
- **Hazards**: Nor'westers (Kalbaishakhi), Heat Wave, Pre-monsoon Drought.
- **Critical Stage**: Jute sowing, Aus vegetative growth.

### 2. Kharif-II (Monsoon: Mid-June to Mid-Oct)
- **Primary Crops**: Aman Rice (Transplant & Broadcast), Jute, Aus Harvest.
- **Hazards**: Flash Flood, Riverine Flood, Tropical Cyclone, Heavy Rainfall.
- **Critical Stage**: Aman transplanting (July), Tillering (Aug), Flowering (Sept).

### 3. Rabi (Winter: Mid-Oct to Mid-Feb)
- **Primary Crops**: Boro Rice, Potato, Wheat, Pulses (Chickpea, Lentil), Oilseeds.
- **Hazards**: Cold Wave, Frost, Winter Drought, Salinity Intrusion (Coastal).
- **Critical Stage**: Boro seedling (Nov), Potato tuber formation (Dec-Jan).
